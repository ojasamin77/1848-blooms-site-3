Questions we hear a lot

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, we recommend reaching out 6–12 months ahead, especially for popular dates. For smaller events, 4–8 weeks is a good starting point. Have something coming up sooner? Reach out and we’ll see what we can do.

How much do wedding and event flowers cost?

Every event is different, so pricing depends on the flowers, arrangements and overall design. Share a few details about your celebration and we’ll put together a proposal based on what you need.

Can I choose my flowers and colors?

Absolutely. We love hearing what you have in mind. We’ll work with your preferred colors and flowers, while recommending seasonal blooms and combinations that work beautifully together. Specific varieties depend on availability.

Do you deliver and set up?

Delivery and on-site setup can be included depending on your event and floral needs. We’ll confirm the arrangements, location and any associated fees in your proposal.

Where do you provide floral services?

We serve Delaware, Pennsylvania and beyond, including Philadelphia, New Hope, Lumberville, Doylestown and Kennett Square. Planning something farther away? We’d love to hear about it.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

We’ll review your event details, learn more about what you’re looking for and discuss the next steps. Once the scope and pricing are agreed upon, we’ll explain the booking and payment process.
